What RRR Is

Knowledge and planning support, built for people navigating this from the inside.

RRR's founding leverage is direct contact and real relationships — with the organizations, employers, and systems that actually shape what reentry looks like — not just information about them.

The Planning Tool

Turn what you did inside into a plan someone outside can read.

The RRR planning tool translates institutional work and responsibility into civilian job language, builds a resume and application package, tracks where you've applied, and maps out what to do first, next, and after that — all in one place, free, no account required.

The Repository Framework

Six steps. Same sequence whether you're planning from inside or already out.

Not a five-year plan. A real first target, and a way to build toward it with whatever you have access to right now.

1

Decide

A real, specific direction for your first 90 days — not a dream, not a five-year plan.

2

Learn

Become credible enough to take the next real step — not an expert in everything.

3

Practice

Turn knowledge into reps. Skill comes from repetition, not understanding alone.

4

Prove

Turn private progress into something someone else can see and trust.

5

Connect

Stop building alone. Let real people become part of how you move forward.

6

Sell

Communicate clearly what you can do, and why it matters to the person deciding.

Make the system visible. Make the path clearer.
